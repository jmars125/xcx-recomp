# xcx60 (XCX_FPS60, on): the frame rate of the frame limit XCX_FPS_LIMIT (frame_rate.c). The interval and period
# sites are our own; the others are ported from the 60FPS mod for 1.0.1 by Fl4sh_ and StevensND

# patch replaces, then in 2.0.0 by the surrounding instructions (unique match); the original words below are 2.0.0's. The mod's other sites (constant time scales at
# +0x1e245c..+0x1e24dc and +0xeb108..+0xeb128) have no clear 1.0.0 counterpart and are not ported.

# The 3D render scale (issue #33). The game runs dynamic resolution: each frame main+0x97730c compares the time from
# main+0x13a28dc with a target and steps a level from 0 to 10, which moves the scale of its record (renderer +0x528) from
# a maximum (+0x14, +0x18) to a minimum (+0x1c, +0x20), all read from /monolib/shader/lib_nx.ini. Then the renderer
# (main+0x971c00) has main+0x9dc89c take the current scale, the record's floats at +0x24 (width) and +0x28 (height), cut
# to whole percents; with scaling off in the record it is 1, with main+0x9dc980 as the exit. The scene draws in that part
# of its full-size targets, and the game's FSR pass scales it up.
# scene: both pointers go to the patch page's +0x58, one float, the scale XCX_RENDER_SCALE asks for. x8 and x9 are
# what the next instructions load through, so nothing else reads them.

# a scaled size up to a multiple of 8, past the target at a height of 900 or 1800.
